Scan: all 14 tokenizers loaded with AutoTokenizer (none skipped; gated ones loaded with the token). Table: `scan.csv`, `scan.md`; cut characters: `scan_cut_chars.csv`.
Cut at combining marks (84.2% of Devanagari words split right before a mark): 7 of 14 tokenizers (Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen3-1.7B, Llama-3.1-8B, Llama-3.2-1B, SmolLM3-3B, Phi-4, OLMo-3-7B). That is 3 distinct merge lists: Qwen, Llama-3 (also SmolLM3), cl100k-style (Phi-4, OLMo-3). So it is NOT Qwen-only, but it is not general: Mistral-Nemo, DeepSeek-V3, o200k (their regex letter classes include \p{M}), Gemma-3, XLM-R, mT5, IndicBERTv2 do not cut at marks (their 6.6-6.8% splits are attached punctuation). Of 6 byte-level regex lineages, 3 cut and 3 do not.
